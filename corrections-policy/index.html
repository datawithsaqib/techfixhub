Corrections Policy

Last updated: 6 Nov 2026

We aim to keep every guide correct and up to date. When we get something wrong, we fix it — and we are grateful when readers tell us.

How to report a mistake

Email datawithsaqib@gmail.com with the page address (URL), what is wrong, and — if you know it — what the correct step is. You can also use the Contact page.

What happens next

  1. We check the report against the current software and official support guidance.
  2. If the report is correct, we fix the guide promptly.
  3. For a material fix — a wrong step, a changed Settings path, or a fix that no longer works — we update the article’s “Last reviewed” date.
  4. Small fixes, such as typos, are corrected without changing the date.

What we correct

We correct wrong steps, outdated Settings paths, broken links, and unclear wording. We also update guides when a software update changes how something works.

We do not remove a guide just because a reader disagrees with it, and we do not change a guide to promote a product. If you think a guide is wrong, tell us why — with the correct step if you can — and we will check it.
