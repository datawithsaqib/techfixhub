Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 Nov 2026

TechFixHub is a small, static website. We do not run accounts, logins, or comments, and we do not ask you to create a profile to read our guides.

What we collect

  • Emails you send us. If you email us at datawithsaqib@gmail.com, we receive your email address and what you wrote. We use it only to reply and to fix the guide you reported. We do not sell it or share it.
  • Basic server logs. Our web host may keep standard logs (such as pages visited and browser type) for security and to keep the site working. We do not use these logs to identify individual readers.

What we do not collect

We do not collect passwords, payment details, or location data. There are no ads on this site yet, so no advertising cookies are set. The search box runs in your browser and does not send your search to us.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not currently use analytics or advertising cookies. If we add analytics or ads later, we will update this page first and explain what is collected.

Links to other sites

Our guides link to official support pages, such as Microsoft Support. Those sites have their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for their content or practices.

Children

This site is written for adults and older readers who use a computer. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the “Last updated” date above. For questions, contact us at datawithsaqib@gmail.com.
