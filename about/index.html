About TechFixHub

TechFixHub is a small technology help publisher. We write clear, step-by-step answers to everyday tech questions — starting with Windows 11 help and software tutorials.

Our readers are in the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, and other English-speaking countries. They come here with a problem: a slow PC, Wi-Fi that stopped working, or an app they cannot figure out. Our job is to give them the fix, in order, in simple English.

How we work

  • Quick answer first. Every guide starts with the short answer, so you do not have to read the whole page to get help.
  • Safe fixes first. Quick and safe steps come before steps that change settings or delete data.
  • Warnings before risky steps. If a step can delete saved passwords or change your network, we warn you before it.
  • Sources. Windows steps follow Microsoft support guidance, and each article lists its sources.
  • Reviewed, not just published. When software changes, we review the guide and update the “Last reviewed” date.

Read our Editorial Policy and Corrections Policy for the full rules.

Who writes the guides?

Guides are written by Saqib. Found a mistake or a step that no longer works? Tell us — we fix errors and say what changed.
